1. THE DAY
----------

Every day is worth exactly 100 XP, for everybody, whatever they track.

XP is experience points. It is the only currency in the app: a day earns up to
100 of it, a week adds its days, a season adds its weeks.

The 100 is split across four categories by fixed weights (section 2). Inside a
category, the habits share it equally. A category with nothing to judge that
day is dropped, and its weight is shared out among the categories that remain,
so the day is still worth exactly 100.

A day's percentage and its XP are therefore the same number. 66 out of 100 is
66% and 66 XP. There is one currency.

Every card on Today says what that habit is worth today and how much of it you
have taken — "18 of 23 XP" — and the cards always add up to the day. Under the
day's total is the distance to a perfect one: "7 XP from a perfect day."

The day boundary is 4am (dayStartHour), not midnight, so the small hours
belong to the night before.

                                            score.js — dayScore, categoryScores


2. THE FOUR CATEGORIES
----------------------

  Core fitness       40     Steps, Workouts
  Discipline         30     Vape puffs, Locked apps
  Rest & recovery    15     Sleep
  Savings            15     Savings

The weights are the group's and nobody can change their own.

Inside a category, habits are averaged. Two Discipline habits means each is
worth half of 30. Adding a habit to a category does not add XP — it splits
the XP already there.

Renormalisation: if Savings has nothing to judge (a monthly goal mid-month),
its 15 is shared among the other three in proportion to their weights, which
is why Core fitness is often shown as "of 47" rather than "of 40":
40 / (40+30+15) = 47%. The day is still out of 100.

                                       score.js — CATEGORY_WEIGHT, categoryScores


3. HOW ONE HABIT IS SCORED
--------------------------

Every habit-period (a day, a week, or a month, depending on the habit) ends in
one of four states:

  HIT       met the goal
  MISS      did not
  NO_DATA   an automatic source reported nothing
  EXEMPT    a rest day, travel, or a grace token spent

Only HIT and MISS are scored. NO_DATA and EXEMPT are left out of the
denominator entirely — they cost nothing and earn nothing.

Silence means different things depending on who was supposed to report:

  Automatic habit, nothing reported  ->  NO_DATA   (a watch that stopped is
                                                    not a person who failed)
  Manual habit, nothing entered      ->  MISS      (the number existed; you
                                                    did not report it)

This is the rule that makes "just never open the app" not a strategy.

Direction decides how the number turns into a score:

  FLOOR  (at least)   score = value / target, capped at 1.15
  CEILING (at most)   over the limit      -> 0, no partial credit
                      exactly at it       -> 1.00
                      under it            -> between 1.00 and 1.15, in
                                             proportion to how far under
                      nothing at all      -> 1.15 (the full bonus)

A typed number overrules every sensor for its day. That is how you correct a
watch that over-counted. It is also permanent for that day unless you take it
back (section 11).

Each person sets their own target. The group agrees on WHAT is tracked; the
number is yours. A target change starts counting from the next day. A first
target counts from the day it is set.

                          score.js  — habitScore


4. THE SIX HABITS, ONE BY ONE
-----------------------------

These are the six the group competes on. Everything else you might track
shows on Today and keeps a streak, but moves nobody's score (section 12).


4.1  STEPS
     Category      Core fitness
     Cadence       daily
     Direction     floor (at least)
     Seed target   10 000, adjusted in steps of 500
     Source        Health Connect on a phone that has it; typed by hand
                   otherwise
     Aggregation   LAST — the provider re-reports the day's running total
                   on every poll, so the newest reading wins
     Silence       NO_DATA if your source is Health Connect; MISS if you
                   log it by hand
     Bonus         yes — walking past the target earns up to 15% extra
     Badges        14 / 30 / 60 / 120 days unmissed

     Health Connect de-duplicates across providers when it aggregates, so a
     phone and a watch describing the same walk count it once. The sync
     reports which apps wrote the day's steps and when, so a low number can
     be traced to its source. If only the handset's own counter is writing,
     the app says so — that means Samsung Health is not writing into Health
     Connect and steps are only being counted while the phone is on you.

                                     HealthConnectSource.kt — readDay, stepOrigins


4.2  SLEEP
     Category      Rest & recovery
     Cadence       daily
     Direction     floor (at least)
     Seed target   7h 00m (420 minutes), adjusted in quarter hours
     Source        Health Connect if a watch reported a night; otherwise the
                   phone's own estimate; typed by hand only if you have
                   deliberately bound it to yourself
     Aggregation   LAST
     Silence       NO_DATA
     Bonus         NO. Rest & recovery is the one category that earns no
                   bonus XP. Sleeping 15% past your target is not an
                   achievement, and paying for it would make "set a low
                   sleep goal" the easiest way up the board.
     Badges        14 / 30 / 60 / 120 days unmissed

     The phone estimate: the longest stretch the phone was left alone that
     covers 3am, between 3 and 14 hours long, measured from screen-off to the
     first UNLOCK (not the first time the screen lit up — a glance at the
     clock or a snooze does not end the night). Attributed to the day you
     woke up. A real reading from a watch is never overwritten by the
     estimate. If neither exists, nothing is reported and the day is
     NO_DATA.

     There is no button to type sleep in. Nobody knows how long they slept
     to the minute, and a guess replacing a measurement is a worse number
     wearing the same badge.

                  SleepEstimate.kt, QuietGap.kt, PauseSource.kt, score.js


4.3  VAPE PUFFS
     Category      Discipline
     Cadence       daily
     Direction     ceiling (at most)
     Seed target   80, adjusted in steps of 5
     Source        typed by hand — nothing can read a vape
     Aggregation   SUM — puffs are counted as they happen, a tap at a time
     Silence       MISS. The vape keeps the count; not entering it is an
                   unreported day, not an unknowable one.
     Bonus         yes — the further under the limit, the more, up to 15%
     Taper         optional. Lowers the limit by 1 every 7 days until it
                   reaches 0. Held for a week when you miss 3 days of the
                   week before — and while held, NO bonus is earned on any
                   habit (section 5).
     Badges        14 / 30 / 60 / 120 days unmissed

     Zero is a real entry. "None today" logs a zero, and a logged zero is a
     HIT; nothing logged is a MISS. They are different days.

     Because the score is SUM, you can only ever add. A mistyped 30 cannot be
     corrected downward by logging — take it back (section 11) and re-enter.

     A ceiling you have never used pays its maximum bonus every day, because
     zero is as far under the limit as it goes. That lifts the Discipline
     average for anybody who tracks this and does not vape. The scoring has
     not been changed for this; instead the board names any habit somebody
     has never missed over a meaningful stretch, so the group can see it.

                                          score.js — habitScore; logsheet.js


4.4  LOCKED APPS
     Category      Discipline
     Cadence       daily
     Direction     ceiling (at most)
     Seed target   2h 00m (120 minutes), adjusted in quarter hours
     Source        the Pause shell on this phone — the accessibility service
                   that raises the breathe screen
     Aggregation   LAST
     Silence       reported as 0 when the phone has been in use and no
                   slowed app was opened; NO_DATA only if the service was
                   not running
     Bonus         yes — time left under the limit earns up to 15%
     Taper         optional, same rule as Vape puffs
     Badges        14 / 30 / 60 / 120 days unmissed

     It counts minutes inside the apps you asked to be slowed — never the
     whole phone. A map, a book or a call costs nothing. Somebody who slows
     nothing is not winning it; they are not playing it.

     Minutes are counted per VISIT, and a visit survives hopping out: leave
     Instagram for WhatsApp and come back inside 90 seconds and the clock
     resumes rather than restarting. Going out through Recents or the shade
     and coming straight back is the same. A keyboard appearing is never
     "leaving". The screen going off ends the leg.

                            AppBlockerService.kt, Visit.kt, Overlay.kt, Prefs.kt


4.5  WORKOUTS
     Category      Core fitness
     Cadence       WEEKLY
     Direction     floor (at least)
     Seed target   3 a week
     Source        Health Connect exercise sessions of 10 minutes or more;
                   or typed by hand
     Aggregation   SUM of discrete sessions, de-duplicated on the session id
                   so a re-poll never counts one workout twice
     Silence       JUDGED, even for an automatic source. A week with no
                   workouts is behind pace, not an outage — you can always
                   log a workout by hand, so a broken watch does not excuse
                   the one habit you could most easily have reported.
     Bonus         yes
     Badges        4 / 12 / 26 / 52 weeks unmissed

     Scored on PACE from Monday, in whole units, rounded UP: on Wednesday
     (day 3 of 7) you are expected to be at ceil(3 x 3/7) = 2, and scored
     against that. Behind pace on Tuesday is the point of running a week. Finish
     the three early and the week holds its maximum for the rest of it —
     three by Wednesday is three, and being asked to keep proving it until
     Sunday would make an early finish worth less than a late one.

     A weekly habit contributes ONE goal to the week's "goals met" count,
     not seven.

     If somebody also tracks Calories burned, the Workouts history screen
     shows the calories inside each week — as context, never as a score.

                        HealthConnectSource.kt — MIN_WORKOUT_MINUTES; score.js


4.6  SAVINGS
     Category      Savings
     Cadence       MONTHLY
     Direction     floor (at least)
     Seed target   1 000, adjusted in steps of 100
     Source        typed by hand
     Aggregation   LAST — a balance, not a sum; logging sets the total
     Silence       not judged until the month closes (see below)
     Bonus         yes
     Badges        3 / 6 / 12 / 24 months unmissed  (HABIT_TIERS.month)

     NOT JUDGED while the month is still running. Money arrives as a payday
     lump, not a daily drip, and a straight-line pace would zero the
     category for the three weeks before anybody is paid. So there is no
     penalty while the month can still be saved, and Savings shows as
     "counts at month end" with its 15 XP shared among the others.

     Two exceptions to "not judged":
       - hit the target early and the month holds its maximum from that day
       - once the month is OVER, the outcome colours EVERY day in it at once.
         A month you never saved in is a MISS on all of its days, not on
         the last one.

     The second is what stops "wait and see" from being free: nothing is
     lost by waiting, and nothing is gained by it either.

                                                        score.js — habitScore


5. BONUS
--------

Beating a target earns up to 15% on top of the habit's score (BONUS_CAP =
1.15). Inside a category the bonus is real — it is what lets a strong Steps
day rescue a soft Workouts week within Core fitness.

The day's displayed score is capped at 100. What beating targets earned on
top is shown SEPARATELY as "+N", so the number everybody reads means what
everybody agreed a day is worth. The week and the season sum the bonus the
same way — beside the total, never inside it.

Bonus stays inside its category. A category's XP is capped at its share
of the day, and only the excess above that is banked as bonus. A big Steps
day cannot pay for a blown Locked-apps day. Inside a category, one habit's
overshoot can cover a sibling's shortfall — Steps at 115% and Workouts at 85%
average to a full Core fitness.

Bonus POINTS are paid in Core fitness, Discipline and Savings. Not in Rest &
recovery — see Sleep above.

Bonus is forfeit for the whole week, across every habit, when a TAPER is
held. A taper holds when you missed 3 days of the previous week. Without a
taper switched on, missing days costs those days and nothing more.

                              score.js — BONUS_CAP, BONUS_CATEGORIES, dayScore
                              habits.js — TAPER_MISS_LIMIT, bonusForfeited


6. THE WEEK
-----------

A week is the SUM of its days' scores, out of 700. Monday to Sunday (ISO
weeks). The season strip on the board can start on a different weekday; the
week being scored does not.

It is a total, not an average, because a league scores appearances. Two
perfect days used to outrank seven near-perfect ones, and a watch that went
quiet was a scoring advantage; a total settles both — 200 does not beat 693.

The cost is stated rather than hidden: a day that earned nothing adds
nothing. A rest day, travel, or a sensor that said nothing all add zero. They
are still not MISSES — they do not break a streak and they are not held
against you — but they do not earn either. Showing up is worth XP on its
own.

The board row:
  460 XP +22         the week's total, and beside it the week's bonus
  92 a day           the average, in the small print
  13 of 15 goals met every scored habit in every period that closed this
                     week: one habit, one period, one goal. A daily habit
                     contributes up to seven; a weekly one contributes one.
  🔥 21 best run     the longest run going on any one habit
  🛡 spent 1         grace tokens spent this week
  4 not reported     habit-periods that were NO_DATA. Shown, and under a
                     total they earned nothing, so the row offers the fix.

The bar is the share of the XP available so far this week that this
person has taken: 100 for every day of the week that has begun, whoever you
are. A full bar is a hundred on every day so far.

Ranking: by XP; then by the average, so two people level on XP are
separated by who did it in fewer days; then by best streak; then by name.
The top row wears the crown. Nothing marks the bottom row — the board is
ranked, and last place is visible without a costume.

"Never missed — Vape puffs 47 days" names a habit this person has never
once missed over at least 14 days / 6 weeks / 3 months. Shown, not scored,
for everybody including you.

                               score.js — scoreOver, leaderboard; dashboard.js


7. THE SEASON
-------------

A season is a stretch of whole weeks. Every completed week adds its XP
to your total, so a week is worth between 0 and 700 and a season runs to
thousands. Bonus is tallied beside it in its own column. This is the running
tally: it only ever goes up, and it keeps going up across seasons.

Season standings are on POINTS. Crowns (weeks won) break a tie. It used to
rank on crowns alone; three near-misses were worth exactly nothing under
that rule.

Scoring starts at the first WHOLE week. The stub before it is a warm-up.
A chosen length always delivers that many whole weeks. Only one season is
active at a time; booking a new one replaces a pending one and does not
erase a finished one — every past season is readable from the strip on the
board.

                                                 season.js — seasonTally, endFor


8. STREAKS AND GRACE
--------------------

A streak is consecutive habit-periods that were HIT or EXEMPT. A MISS with
nothing banked resets it to zero. NO_DATA neither breaks it nor extends it.

The period still running is never a miss — a streak survives the morning.

Grace tokens: earn one every 7 clean days (daily habits; 4 weeks for
weekly, 3 months for monthly). Hold at most 2 (1 for weekly and monthly). A
MISS with a token in the bank spends the token and becomes EXEMPT: the day
drops out of scoring and the streak survives. Shown on the board as
"spent 1".

This is earned insurance, which is why it is not exploitable — you paid a
premium of seven clean days for it.

The group streak (the flame on Today) counts days on which EVERY habit was
on goal. Its tiers are the four group badges below.

                                habits.js — walk; schema.js — GRACE_BY_PERIOD


9. BADGES
---------

Group tiers, on the all-habits streak:
     Bronze     7 days     Silver    20 days
     Gold      50 days     Diamond  100 days

Per-habit levels, in the habit's own cadence:
     Daily      14 / 30 / 60 / 120 days
     Weekly      4 / 12 / 26 /  52 weeks
     Monthly     3 /  6 / 12 /  24 months

Badges count CROSSINGS, so one can be won twice. Every rung is drawn, won or
not — a ladder you cannot see the top of is not a ladder.

10. DAYS THAT DO NOT COUNT
--------------------------

  Rest days       a daily habit scheduled on Mon/Wed/Fri is EXEMPT on the
                  other days
  Travel          booked from today onwards, never backdated. Ending a trip
                  early may only bring the last day forward. Notifications
                  go quiet for the trip and resume after.
  Grace spent     section 8
  NO_DATA         section 3
  The open day    never judged until it closes
  A month running section 4.6

An exempt period preserves a streak and is never a MISS. Under the week's
total it earns nothing either — a day you did not play adds zero, whatever
the reason. A week is exempt only when every day in it is.

                                habits.js — rawPeriodStatus; travelsheet.js


11. WHAT THE LOG WILL NOT LET YOU DO
------------------------------------

The log is append-only. Nothing is ever deleted; every phone replays the
same events to the same state.

  A log more than 2 days late is refused. Last week's crown is not
  winnable on Tuesday.

  One exception, cut narrow: a STEPS habit kept BY HAND (bound to manual,
  no watch) may be entered for an earlier day of the SAME week. A watch
  backfills a week on its own when it reconnects; a person without one has
  only the evening they remember. Never across a Monday, never for a habit
  fed by a sensor, never for any other metric. On the entry sheet, hold the
  habit's name for a moment and the week's days appear. It is not
  advertised anywhere else.

  Travel cannot be backdated at all.

  A target change counts from tomorrow. Lowering the number tonight does
  not rescue today. A WEEKLY goal is read at the start of the week, so a
  change made mid-week counts from Monday, and a monthly one from the 1st.
  Until then the card shows both numbers: "of 2 this week" and, under it,
  "Goal → 3 from Mon, Sep 14".

  Opting out of a habit is judged as of when you opted out; it does not
  excuse the days before.

  Every guard reads min(claimed time, server arrival), so a phone with its
  clock set forward cannot buy anything.

  A typed number can be TAKEN BACK for that day. This appends a withdrawal
  rather than deleting anything, and only removes what you typed — the
  sensor's own reading underneath takes over again. It is subject to the
  same 2-day window as a log.

                        habits.js — replay, MAX_BACKFILL_DAYS, authoredAt
                        schema.js — clearLog


12. PERSONAL WORKOUT PROGRAMS
-----------------------------

Two programs, written for two people, live in the app as data: Match Fit and
Rope Protocol. Follow one from the Workouts card ("Follow a program"). The
card then opens the program: today's suggested session at the top, every
session below it, and the rest days named. Programs are not edited in the
app; when a plan changes, the file changes.

THE SCHEDULE IS A SUGGESTION. Any session can be started on any day. It is
logged as that session on the day it was actually done, and the next time
that session opens it prefills from it — so Monday's session done on a
Wednesday is not lost, not penalised, and not confused with Wednesday.

Every exercise carries its how-to steps, a cue and a watch-for. The steps
open on the first set of each exercise in a session and fold after; the cue
and the watch-for stay on the active exercise throughout, because the second
is a safety line as often as a form line. Each program's before-you-start
notes are on the way in, and the warm-up is on screen until the first set.

A session is logged one set at a time. Each set opens on what you did last
time in that set — the first time ever, on the program's floor — so Done is
the whole interaction when nothing changed and + then Done when you beat it.
Banking a set starts the rest clock. Everything banked survives the app
closing, until you tap Finish.

Rope days (Rope Protocol) are intervals: work, rest and rounds that step up
by week, counted from the program's start date of 2 Sep 2026. The timer runs
the low end of that week's range until you bump it. A core finisher follows
on the ordinary sets screen; the rope day is one entry in the history.

FINISH COUNTS ON THE BOARD. Finishing a session writes one workout to your
Workouts habit for that day, with an id built from the day and the session,
so finishing twice corrects the sets and does not count two workouts. A watch
that caught the same session is a separate row, as it always was. A typed
number overrules a sensor for its day, so on a day you also ran, the watch's
2 shows as the app's 1 — enter the second one by hand if it matters.

Tennis is a rest day the schedule names on purpose. Nothing is logged against
it.

History is per exercise, on the Workouts habit's history screen: the last few
sessions as "10 · 10 · 9" chips with the newest lit, and whether the latest
total is up, down or the same as the one before. The trend is on the TOTAL,
so adding a set is progress even when it is a short one.

PERSONAL BESTS. Each exercise keeps its best single set (the record) and its
best session total, with the day each was set. On the session screen every
exercise shows last time and the record beside its tiles — "Last 10 · 10 · 9
· PB 10 reps (Mon)". A set that is strictly greater than the record turns its
tile amber, marks it PB, buzzes differently, and writes "New personal best"
under the card. Matching the record is not beating it. Records are read when
the session opens and not moved until it is finished, so two sets in one
session that both beat the old record are both celebrated — which is what
they both did.

YOUR TRAINING is shown on the program's front page and on the Insights tab
(the same numbers, worked out once): sessions, weeks in a row, sets banked,
reps lifted, minutes held, rope rounds; then, once there are 3 sessions to
go on, the favourite exercise (finished in full most often), the least
favourite (cut short most often — not named when everything is always
finished), the most improved (latest session total against the first), the
weekday sessions actually land on (only when one day clearly leads), and the
longest rope interval. "Weeks in a row" counts back from this week, and a
week that has not had its session YET is not a gap. None of this is scored;
the board only ever sees the finished workout.

A session swiped away half-done is not lost. Every set banked stays in
the draft until Finish; the program's front page marks that session "In
progress · 4 of 15 sets" and the Workouts habit's detail offers "Continue".
The front page can be opened on any day, rest days included — the schedule
suggests, it never locks a door.

A workout more than 2 days late is refused, like a log.

              programs.js, workout.js, workoutsheet.js, store.js, summary.js


13. LEVELS — LIFETIME XP
------------------------

Every day you play adds its XP to a lifetime total: the same 0–100 the
board scores for the day, plus what beating your goals earned, as one
number. Every day since you joined, across every season. Nothing new is
invented and nothing new can be gamed — a day is still worth at most 115,
and an unreported day still adds nothing.

IT ONLY GOES UP. A bad week costs nothing already earned. A level once
reached is never lost.

DAYS BANK WHEN THEY CLOSE. Today's XP can still fall (a ceiling habit can
turn a hit into a miss at eleven at night), so today is drawn as a faint tip
on the bar and is not in the level until midnight. A level reached at six
and lost by eleven cannot happen.

THE CURVE. Each level asks 25 XP more than the one before, starting at 300:
Level 2 at 300, Level 3 at 625, Level 10 at 3,600, Level 20 at 9,975, Level
50 at 44,100, Level 100 — the last — at 150,975. At 85 a day that is Level 2
on day three, 10 in six weeks, 20 in four months, 50 in a year and a half,
100 in about five years. Late levels are nine times rarer than early ones.

TITLES, one per ten levels: Starter · Regular (10) · Steady (20) · Solid
(30) · Committed (40) · Relentless (50) · Veteran (60) · Iron (70) ·
Unbroken (80) · Master (90) · Legend (100).

RECOGNITION, NOT POWER. Levels unlock nothing in the scoring and there is
no lifetime leaderboard — somebody who joined a year later would never
catch up, and the league lives on the week and the season. The level shows
as a ring (never a medal: medals are streaks) beside your name on the web
header, on every board row, and on the Insights header with the bar and
the sentence "1,100 XP to Level 8". The ring and the bar draw THIS level —
from where it began to where the next begins — and start again at every
level; today's XP is the faint tip. The lifetime total is its own tile
beside the days played and the rate. Ten pips under the bar mark which
level of the current title you are on. Reaching a level opens a one-time
screen the next morning.

ABOUT YOU. Tap your own name on Insights (or the level chip on the web) and
the record says what it can about you: days played, your best day and best
week, perfect days, the weekday you are strongest on (and softest, when the
gap is real), how far you have walked and how long you have slept since you
joined, the vape ceiling that has come down, days under your screen limit,
workouts and personal bests, money put away, the habit you have never
missed, the "worth noticing" comparison, where you stand this season, and
when the next level and title land at your pace. Every fact has a floor
below which it is simply absent. Nothing here is scored, and nothing here
reaches the board or the group.
